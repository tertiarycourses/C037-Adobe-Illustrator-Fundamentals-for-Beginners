## About the course

This repository contains the learner-facing v1 courseware for Adobe Illustrator Fundamentals for Beginners. The course guides you through the core features of Adobe Illustrator — vector graphics creation, logo design and typography — and suits both beginners and those looking to refine their skills. The slide deck is concept-led and highly visual; detailed procedures, evidence and acceptance criteria are kept in the Learner Guide and the individual lab folders.

## Learning outcomes

By the end of the course, learners will be able to:

- apply basic Illustrator techniques to create digital graphics;
- improve Illustrator graphics through controlled experimentation; and
- evaluate and output digital graphics against visual, technical and rights criteria.

## Topics covered

- **Topic 1 — Basic Digital Graphics Design with Illustrator:** vector foundations, workspace, artboards, selection, shapes, colour, strokes, layers and transforms.
- **Topic 2 — Improve Digital Graphics Design with Illustrator:** construction methods, guides and grids, pen and brush systems, gradients, patterns, symbols, appearance and typography.
- **Topic 3 — Evaluate Graphics Design with Illustrator:** linked imagery, Image Trace, artboards, output formats, package and preflight, intellectual property and peer review.
