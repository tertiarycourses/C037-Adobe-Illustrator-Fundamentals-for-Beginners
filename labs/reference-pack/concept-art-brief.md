# Concept-Art Brief — Emberdrift

*The shared example project for all 9 labs. Fictional — use it, or substitute
your own non-confidential project at any point.*

## The game

**Emberdrift** is a (fictional) indie adventure game from the (fictional) studio
**Northlight Studios**. It is set on a world of **floating islands** drifting
above an endless **sea of clouds**. Young **sky-salvagers** ride patched gliders
between lantern-lit sky-ports, scavenging relic-tech from ruins that rise through
the cloud sea. The tone is **hopeful and adventurous** — warmth and wonder, not
grimdark.

## The hero character — Wren

- A **determined teenage sky-salvager**, wiry and agile.
- A **patched leather flight coat**, goggles pushed up on the forehead, a coil of
  rope and salvage tools at the belt, worn boots.
- Personality: resourceful, curious, quick to grin — hopeful, not hardened.

## The key environment — the sky-port

- A **floating lantern sky-port at dusk**: wooden docks and rope bridges strung
  between drifting islands above the cloud sea.
- Patched gliders moored, **hundreds of warm lanterns** glowing, a tall
  **salvage-crane** landmark for scale.
- Warm amber light against a cool, misty blue sky.

## The props — Wren's gear

| Prop | Description |
|---|---|
| **Grappling lantern-hook** | A brass hook with a small glowing lantern housing and a coiled rope |
| **Wind-compass** | A brass compass with a spinning vane and a small pressure dial |
| **Goggles** | Leather-and-brass flight goggles with amber-tinted lenses |

Worn, functional, brass-and-leather — read clearly on a plain background.

## The look (house style)

- **Painterly game concept art**, cinematic, digital-painting feel.
- **Warm amber lantern light** against **cool misty blues and greys**.
- **Golden-dusk** atmosphere, volumetric light, atmospheric depth.
- Hopeful, adventurous mood — a world you want to explore.

## Guardrails (commercial-use & IP)

- Do **not** prompt for any real company's trademark, a living artist's name, or
  copyrighted characters.
- Use only reference images you are authorised to use.
- Emberdrift and Northlight Studios are fictional — any resemblance to a real
  game or studio is coincidental; do not use them commercially.
- Check your **tool's** commercial-use terms (Firefly is commercial-safe and
  indemnified; Midjourney and Stable Diffusion vary by plan and model), and
  disclose AI assistance where appropriate before publishing or selling. (Full
  checklist in Lab 9.)
