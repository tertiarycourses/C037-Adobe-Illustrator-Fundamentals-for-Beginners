# Labs — Generative AI for Concept Art (C037)

**Course Code:** C037  |  **Version v1.0 · 27 July 2026**

All 9 labs build one connected **Emberdrift concept-art pitch pack**, which you begin in Lab 1 and finish in Lab 9 — from a text prompt, through prompting, character, environment and prop generation with Midjourney, Stable Diffusion and Adobe Firefly, into a locked house style, refined iterations, inpainting, outpainting, upscaling and a hand paint-over, and out as a curated, presented portfolio. An Emberdrift concept-art brief is supplied in `reference-pack/`; use your own non-confidential project wherever you prefer. There is **no assessment** — each lab verifies itself with a 'Test it' step.
